## Review Cadence

This register must be reviewed at each Technology Roadmap checkpoint (`roadmap/technology-roadmap.md` Year 1/2/3 milestones) and whenever a named migration trigger (`migration/migration-and-upgrade-strategy.md`) fires — not on a fixed calendar alone.
